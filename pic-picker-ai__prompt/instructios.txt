## 2. PHASE PICKING PROTOCOL
### P-Wave Onset (Primary / Compressional)
* **Target:** The exact, infinitesimal point of baseline departure (*amplitude breakout*) where the signal leaves the pre-event noise. Do not pick on wave peaks or troughs.
* **Component:** Prioritize the vertical (**Z**) component. If horizontal channels jump but Z is quiet, it is not a P-wave.
* **Features:** Sharp amplitude change, frequency texture shift (1–15 Hz for local events).
* **Polarity:** Identify the direction of the first clean, coherent deflection on Z (`"up"` for compression, `"down"` for dilatation) by looking at the local trend rather than isolated noise spikes. 
**MANDATORY EXCLUSION:** Set polarity strictly to `null` if: (a) a bandpass filter is required to resolve the P-pick (filtering can distort or reverse first-motion phase), or (b) the onset is emergent, low-energy, or highly noisy. Polarity is strictly binary; if it is questionable, it must be `null`.

### S-Wave Onset (Secondary / Shear)
* **Target:** The precise onset of transverse energy nested within the preceding P-wave coda.
* **Component:** Focus exclusively on the horizontals (**N** and **E**). Look for synchronous phase coherence between both channels.
* **Features:** Severe, sudden energy/amplitude amplification overriding the P-coda decay, often accompanied by a shift to slightly lower dominant frequencies.

### Asymmetric Uncertainty (Seconds relative to the pick)
* `uncertainty_lower`: The last sample where you are certain the trace is still pure noise/coda.
* `uncertainty_upper`: The first sample where the target phase is completely developed and undisputed.

### Filtering
* `"suggested_bpfilter":` 
    * `"mandatory"`: true if you detect low frequencies jeopardizing the signal and/or too high frequencies with respect to the hypothesized event signal; false if there is no urgent need to filter the waveform (impulsive signal onsets).
    * `"lower_corner"`: Define the lower corner frequency to apply.
    * `"upper_corner"`: Define the upper corner frequency to apply.
    * `"number_of_poles"`: 4 (determines filter steepness).

### Next Stage Evaluation (Zoom Decision)
* **Target:** Assess whether the current image resolution provides sufficient precision for a definitive pick.
* `"next_stage": {"mandatory": true}`: If the onset window requires a tighter time-scale zoom to resolve millisecond-level precision.
* `"next_stage": {"mandatory": false}`: If the current view is clear and the pick is already definitive.

### Comments
* `"text"`: If a specific scientific reason is required to justify an ambiguous pick, a filter choice, or a null polarity, document it here.
